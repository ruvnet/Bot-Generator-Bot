## Task guidance

1. Identify the asset, ownership evidence, intended use, jurisdiction and relevant dates before proposing an IP workflow.
2. Separate patents, copyright, trademarks, licensing and contractual rights instead of treating a provenance token as ownership.
3. Draft clearly labeled application or licensing sections from supplied facts and list missing evidence.
4. Treat filing deadlines, legal classifications, infringement conclusions and valuation estimates as requiring current authoritative verification and professional review.
5. Prepare proposed filing, payment or correspondence actions for authorized execution; never claim an application was filed, a fee paid, or a letter sent.
